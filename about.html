About Vertisco

The whole picture — seen by someone who's done it before.

Vertisco is an operational intelligence and AI consulting practice built for small and medium-sized businesses.

Twenty years of seeing the whole picture

For over two decades, we worked inside large multinationals in the energy trading and shipping sectors. These are environments where operations are intricate, the stakes are high, and a single inefficiency has a number attached to it.

In those roles, we developed a particular skill: the ability to look at a business end-to-end — to step back, see the whole picture, find where the friction lives, and deliver technology-led solutions that make a real, measurable difference.

Vertisco brings that same capability, and the same rigour, to ambitious independent businesses. Because the problems a fast-growing company faces aren't so different from those of a global trading operation. The systems haven't kept up. The processes are holding things back. Nobody's had the time, or the outside perspective, to fix it properly.

More recently, we've designed and built enterprise-wide systems — complete, AI-powered platforms that replace fragmented tools across an entire operation, end to end.

What we believe

The principles behind every engagement

Integrity

We only take on work we genuinely believe we can deliver well. If we're not the right fit, we'll say so.

Impact

Tangible, measurable improvement is the only result that counts. We don't produce reports that gather dust.

Honesty

We tell you what you need to hear, not what you want to hear. Plain speaking, always.

Passion

If we're not genuinely excited by the problem, we won't do our best work. We choose our engagements accordingly.

Connection

We work with people we respect and enjoy working with. The relationship matters as much as the outcome.

Clarity

We cut through complexity and translate technology into plain language. No jargon. No unnecessary complexity.

The name

Why "Vertisco"?

Vertisco comes from the Latin vertere — to turn, to transform, to change direction. It reflects what we do: we help businesses turn complexity into clarity. It's not a buzzword or an acronym. It's a name that means something.
