How it works

Low-risk to begin.
High-impact in delivery.

Every engagement follows the same three stages — built to last. You can stop after the first stage. Most don't.

01
Stage 01 — Discover

The Discovery Sprint

A structured, independent review of your business, delivered as a clear written report. We examine your processes, systems and workflows end-to-end, then tell you — plainly and honestly — what we found, what it's costing, and what to do about it.

You walk away with the Vertisco Insight Report: a prioritised list of what's holding you back, specific recommendations for each, the relevant AI and technology use-cases, an estimate of the return, and a roadmap showing what to fix first and why.

Even if you go no further, the report is yours to act on.

02
Stage 02 — Deliver

We build the solutions

We turn the findings into reality. Every Delivery engagement is scoped individually from your Insight Report and confirmed in writing before any work begins — so you know exactly what you're getting and what it costs, up front.

AutomationDashboardsIntegrationsRedesigned processes
03
Stage 03 — Retain

Your ongoing partner

The best improvements aren't one-offs. As a Retain client you get a consultant who already knows your business inside out, available each month to advise, optimise and respond as things evolve. No briefing from scratch. No ramp-up. Just continued, compounding value.
