Flagship example

A platform, not a patch.

For an owner-led business outgrowing its off-the-shelf software, we designed and built a complete operations platform from the ground up — a custom web application that replaced their project-management tool entirely.

It brought the whole operation into one place: live dashboards and reporting, automated invoicing, workflow automation, staff and holiday management, and a single source of truth drawn from data that had been scattered across spreadsheets and disconnected tools.

Built with AI as the engine, it was delivered in a fraction of the time and cost of traditional development — and is fully owned by the client.
