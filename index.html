The problem

You know it could run better.

The team is busy, the business is growing — but somewhere along the way the systems stopped keeping pace. Data lives in spreadsheets. Half the process is in someone's head. Nobody has a clear, real-time picture of what's actually happening.

You're not short of ideas or drive. You've just never had the time, the resource, or an outside pair of eyes to fix it properly. That's exactly what we do.

What we do

Three disciplines. One outcome: a business that runs better.

Operational Intelligence

We take a holistic, end-to-end view of your business — mapping how work really flows, and surfacing the hidden inefficiencies quietly costing you time and money.

Process Re-Engineering

We redesign the way work moves through your organisation — cutting duplication and manual effort, and building processes that are robust, repeatable and ready to scale.

AI-Powered Automation

We put practical, proportionate technology to work — using AI and modern tools to do the heavy lifting, so your team can focus on what they do best.

The technology is the means. The product is a better business.

Why Vertisco

We understand the business before we touch the technology.

Most "AI" firms lead with the tools. We lead with your business. For over twenty years, we have diagnosed and fixed operational friction inside large multinationals in the energy trading and shipping sectors — places where a single inefficiency costs real money, fast.

The problems a growing independent business faces aren't so different. The systems haven't kept up. The processes are holding things back. We've spent a career fixing exactly that.

And when the tools themselves are the problem, we go further — designing and building complete, AI-powered platforms that replace an organisation's software entirely.
